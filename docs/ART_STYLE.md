# Art Style (read before building anything visible)
_Goal: bright and friendly, but rich in detail up close. Simple shapes, never plain boxes._

## Light & atmosphere
- Warm sun, soft shadows on; Atmosphere with light haze so far terraces fade slightly (depth).
- ColorCorrection: slightly lower brightness, a bit more contrast and saturation than default; gentle Bloom on gold/neon only.
- Avoid flat white/cream fields: break large floors with tile patterns, borders, darker paths.

## Every building / stall needs
1. A base trim or step (never sits flat on the ground).
2. A roof edge or cornice + one roof detail (awning, sign box, chimney, rooftop prop).
3. Windows or openings with a darker inset (and warm glow at night/inside).
4. Two materials minimum (e.g. plaster + wood, brick + metal), one accent colour.
5. One readable sign. Small props at the door (planter, bench, lamp, crate).

## Ground & edges
- Curbs where surfaces meet, grass edges with small bushes/flowers, rock undersides on islands/terraces.
- Railings with posts, not single bars.

## Colour
- Base: cream, sandstone, warm grey. Accents per zone (Zone 1 red/teal, Zone 6 gold/checkered). Gold = money/rare.
- Max 3 main colours per building.

## Budget
- Reuse masters (one per prop), MeshParts over many small parts, decoration CanCollide off, CastShadow off on tiny props.
