## Goal
Every screen looks like a polished top Roblox simulator: chunky, bright, thick outlines, ribbon headers, big icons, readable on a phone. Players always have a reason to come back (daily, playtime, offline, index).

## Style base: Check-UI (D18)
Reference: https://github.com/TemporaryLooker22/Check-UI-Clean-Roblox at commit `52ac1ed` (MIT per its README). Clone it, read `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` + `src/`, and adapt `CheckUITheme`, `CheckUIComponents`, `CheckUIController` into our `UITheme` / components: 3D bevelled buttons, [X] close button, 2-tier header shadow, button state triad (claim/claimed/locked/skip), checkerboard fade, sunburst behind items, device scaling, centre-anchored UIScale tweens, GothamBlack + thick strokes, per-menu header colours (Shop magenta, Daily lime, Settings silver, Codes cyan; Rebirth purple later).
- Do NOT use their icon registry or any of their `rbxassetid`s (icons, checkerboard, sunburst, sounds): they belong to another account and can disappear. Make our own checkerboard + sunburst textures in Blender and add them to our sprite sheet; sounds from Roblox's licensed library.
- Log the source in `docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md`.

## Phase 1 — UI kit
- Extend the `UITheme` module from 028 into the single source for: palette (cream, gold, charcoal + bright accents), fonts (bold Roblox fonts, e.g. Fredoka One / Luckiest Guy), UIStroke thickness, UICorner radius, UIGradient presets, button press squash, open/close bounce.
- Reusable components: button (primary / secondary / close X), panel with ribbon header, item card, currency pill with "+" button, progress bar, tab bar, toast, popup.
- **Icons (D14):** Claude models ~25–30 icons in headless Blender (cash, gem, shop, backpack, index book, gift, calendar, clock, settings, close, lock, check, rarity stars, trophy, arena, base, parade, 2x, VIP, slots…), renders transparent 256 px PNGs, packs them into 1–2 sprite sheets (≤1024×1024). Original only. Export to `assets/export/ui/`.
- Phone rules: scale with UIScale/AspectRatio, respect safe areas, touch targets ≥ 44 px, nothing covers the thumbstick or jump button.

## Phase 2 — Restyle everything through UITheme
HUD (cash + gem pills top-left, icon buttons left side, goal bar from 028), reveal banner, Golden Parade countdown, collection book, existing shops, arena UI, quests, tutorial prompts. Same look everywhere.

## Phase 3 — V1 screens
1. **Shop / Store**: tabs for Gamepasses (2x Cash, Extra Slots, VIP), Cash Packs, Starter Pack (one-time, good value). Read item IDs from a `ProductConfig` module; IDs are 0 for now → card shows "Coming soon" and is disabled (purchases are wired in 031). No pressure wording ("can't refuse", fake timers, "only X left").
2. **Inventory** grid: restyled, rarity-coloured cards, sort by rarity/income, favourite star.
3. **Index** with completion bonus (D15): page per zone, silhouettes for missing workers, progress bar, bonus tiers shown.
4. **Daily rewards**: 7-day streak calendar, claim button, resets if a day is missed (show this kindly).
5. **Offline earnings** popup on join: "Your workers earned $X while you were away!" Collect button with coin burst.
6. **Playtime rewards**: gifts at e.g. 5, 10, 20, 30, 45, 60 min of the session.
7. **Settings**: music, SFX, low effects, low graphics.
