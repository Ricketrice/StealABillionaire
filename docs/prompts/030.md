## Phase 1 — Royale Race (D21)
- **Join:** glowing start pad at the start line. A race starts every 3 minutes if at least 1 player is on the pad; a short 5-second countdown (big numbers, sound) before the start. Players who step off before "GO" are not in the race.
- **Race:** normal Roblox running on the existing circuit. Racers don't collide with each other (collision group). No abilities, no speed changes, no items.
- **Checkpoints:** invisible checkpoints in order around the track; a finish only counts if every checkpoint was passed in order. No UI for this. Players who skip get no finish.
- **Time limit:** 90 s (tune to the track: ~2x a normal lap). When it ends, unfinished racers get nothing; race resets.
- **Rewards:** every finisher gets one capsule that auto-opens at the finish with the existing reveal; the worker goes to the player like a normal catch (storage if pads are full).
  - Gold for the top 3 finishers, Silver for everyone else who finishes.
  - Only real finishers get rewards; no rewards for empty places.
  - **Small races:** with 1–3 racers only 1st place gets Gold (stops solo servers farming Gold). 4+ racers → top 3 Gold.
- **Reward safety:** server-authoritative. One capsule per player per race, decided and saved once (idempotent, keyed by race ID + player). Survives a disconnect right after finishing; can't be duplicated by re-sending, rejoining or leaving mid-race.
- **Capsule odds:** propose Silver and Gold odds in a `RaceConfig` module, mapped to the Zone 6 worker pool. Gold must be better than Silver; neither guarantees Legendary or Secret; show the odds on the pad sign. Don't change existing Zone 6 catch odds.
- **Podium:** after each race, top 3 names shown on a podium board for 20 s.
- Feature flag `RaceEnabled` (Studio attribute like the others). Flag OFF = Zone 6 behaves exactly as before.
