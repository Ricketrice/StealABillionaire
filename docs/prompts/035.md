## Goal
Every creature has ONE real model. The creature that walks in the parade, wanders in a zone, gets carried, stands on a base pad, shows in the Index and pops in the reveal is **the exact same model**. Common/Rare look like the owner's reference (classic blocky Roblox bodies, each with its own colours and one twist). Epic/Legendary/Secret are beautiful and match their theme.

## Owner decisions (already made)
- **Look reference (owner image):** classic blocky Roblox-style figures in different shirt and pants colours. Use this body for Common and Rare. Differences come from colours, hair/hat, and one twist item.
- **Themed signature, not generic:** knight = holds a sword. Wizard = casts spells (flames from staff or hand). Dragon = breathes fire. Water creatures = bubbles or water jets. Archer = bow. Shield Knight = big shield. Think "what is this creature, show it".
- **Rarest ones must be beautiful.** Epic/Legendary/Secret get custom meshes (Blender), aura, signature idle, scale up.
- IDs, rarities, odds, income, names (from 034) don't change. Only looks.

## LOOK RULE (owner, after play-check of Knight's Keep) — overrides the "blocky R15 avatar" wording above
Owner image: the classic Roblox **noob**. Yellow head with the plain smile face, smooth solid-colour block torso, two block arms, two block legs, no hair, no shading textures, no realistic skin. The current R15 avatar bodies (skin tones, hair, shaded limbs) look bad.
- **EVERY humanoid creature, any rarity (Common to Secret), uses this noob body.** Variety comes from torso/leg/arm colours, hat or helmet, and the twist item. Rare/Epic/Legendary/Secret keep the same body and add armour, robes, capes, wings, glow, aura, bigger scale on top (that is where "beautiful" comes from).
- **Non-humanoid creatures** (fish, owl, snail, newt, seahorse, phoenix, dragons, golem and similar) get their own body from their own shapes, not the noob.
- Default Roblox face on the head. Simple hats are allowed; no realistic hair.
- Keep the Humanoid and the R15 part names so carry, placement and income need no change. Simplest way: hidden R15 parts with visible classic blocks welded on (like Sir Ironclad). This also cuts part counts (a noob is about 7 parts).
- Apply to all 27 (Knight's Keep, Sunken Kingdom and Ember Academy). The look is data-driven, so change the body builder once, then re-run budgets and the creature035 test.
