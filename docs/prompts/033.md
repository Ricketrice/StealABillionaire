## Goal
Bases feel alive and there is always something to spend cash on: **workers visibly working → level them up → buy more pads → earn more.** The game can make money in a fair, kid-safe way: 3 gamepasses, cash packs and a starter pack.

## Phase 1 — Living bases (D25)
- Every placed worker **does a job at its pad**: a short code-driven loop that fits its type (Earner counts cash / types on a laptop, Fighter does push-ups, Tank stands guard, All-rounder sweeps), plus a coin pop when it earns. Same code-driven style as 028 (tweens/CFrames, no uploads).
- Cheap: animate only when within ~80 studs of the camera; "Low effects" = simple bob. Report frame time with 12 full bases.

## Phase 2 — Worker stats, levels, pads
- **Stats + types complete for all 16 workers** (fill gaps in the existing style; don't change existing values, IDs, rarities or base income). Arena stays hidden (D29), so cards show only what matters now: **type icon + $/s + level**. Full stats on tap.
- **Levels 1–10**, bought with cash at the pad or from the card. Each level raises that worker's income (proposal: +10%/level on its base income). Visible change at level 5 (badge) and 10 (gold glow). Level is saved per worker (optional field, schema 3). Stolen workers keep their level.
- **Base pads 7–12** for cash, rising cost, unlocked one at a time at the base (a glowing "Unlock $X" pad). Reuse existing pads 7–8 if they exist; build new ones in the same style if needed, without changing the 2×6 base layout (D27).
- **Feature reveal (D28):** Upgrade buttons appear after the player has 3 workers placed; the next locked pad appears when the current pads are full. Each with a one-time "NEW!" hint.

## Phase 3 — Fair stealing (protects kids)
- **Favourite worker protection:** the starred favourite (one per player) can't be stolen. Shown with a small shield on its pad.
- **Steal limits:** a thief can steal from the same base at most once per X minutes, and a victim loses at most Y workers per Z minutes, then their base gets a short protection bubble. Numbers in `StealConfig` for approval.
- Never shows a "buy" prompt right after being robbed (Never list).

## Phase 4 — Monetization (D13, D19)
- **Gamepasses:** 2x Cash, Extra Slots, VIP. Propose exact perks and Robux prices (design ranges: 2x Cash 300–500, Extra Slots 250–400, VIP 300–600; kid-friendly prices preferred).
  - 2x Cash doubles all worker income. Extra Slots adds pads beyond 12 (or more storage, propose). VIP = cosmetic + small comfort perks (name tag colour, VIP chat tag, small bonus); **no arena gear, no zone unlocks, no PvP advantage.**
- **Cash packs (dev products):** 3 sizes, amount scales with the player's current income (e.g. "X minutes of your income", D20) so they never become useless. They speed up, never skip.
- **Starter pack:** one-time, good value: cash + one exclusive cosmetic (e.g. a trail colour with Bubble's speed, not faster). Shows only for the first days, without fake timers.
- **Group reward:** only if the owner has a Roblox group (ask at the checkpoint); otherwise skip.
- **Purchases are safe:** `ProcessReceipt` grants once (receipt ID stored in a new optional save field, list capped), saves before returning `PurchaseGranted`, retries on failure. Gamepass perks checked with `UserOwnsGamePassAsync` (cached) plus `PromptGamePassPurchaseFinished`. IDs live in `ProductConfig`; ID 0 = card shows "Coming soon" (as in 029).
- No pressure wording, no fake countdowns, no "only X left", no prompt after losing or being robbed (Never list).
