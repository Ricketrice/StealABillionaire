## Phase 2 — Simple, trendy map (everything except Zones 2–5)
**Direction changed (D6):** copy the simplicity of today's top Roblox hits (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot-style games): one clean, flat, bright map a new player understands in 5 seconds. Simple beats detailed. Goal: attract and keep players, especially on phones.

**Layout — one compact island, no water, no skyline**
- Flat grassy/paved island floating in bright sky (void plane below → respawn at spawn; low edge walls where players could fall).
- **Centre strip:** spawn + shops in a row (each shop = a simple colourful stall/booth, one big readable sign), Golden Vault as the one landmark, arena, one "Top Billionaires" board. Absorb or remove the old gate disc and SAFE HUB arch (safe zone = coloured paving).
- **Parade lane:** keep a clear 14–16 stud lane running the length of the centre strip, passing in front of all 12 lots (red-carpet coloured paving, no props in it). Milestone 028 puts walking workers on it; don't build the parade now.
- **Lots:** all 12 in two rows facing the centre strip, so everyone sees everyone's base (that's what makes stealing exciting). Each lot: coloured base pad, owner name sign, low border. Pads, collector, PvP bounds untouched except moving the whole lot.
- **Gates:** Zone 1 at one end of the strip, Zone 6 at the other, simple short paths/bridges to Zones 2–5 entrances (stop at their boundary). Coloured line on the ground to each gate.
- **Carry rule:** Zone 1 exit → farthest lot ≤ ~20 s at carry speed (path length ÷ carry WalkSpeed **and** one timed Play run).

**Style**
- Bright, saturated, cheerful colours (gold, green, sky blue, cream); chunky simple shapes; SmoothPlastic/Plastic mostly, a little Neon/Glass for highlights. Clear silhouettes; readable from a phone.
- No modular facade kit, no Blender, no imports unless a single item truly needs it. Build in Studio.
- Shops/stalls: a counter, a striped awning, one sign, a shopkeeper — that's it.
- Few props, each with a purpose: some trees (simple round canopies on trunks), lamps, benches, a few money/gold decorations. No filler buildings.
- Zone 1 Main Street: keep it, simplify to the same style (simple colourful shopfronts, no heavy detail).
- Zone 6: keep Velocity Royale's layout; only connect it.
