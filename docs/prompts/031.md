## Goal
A big, simple "wow" moment for players and trailers: **the vault opens → grab a capsule → the giant security robot chases you → reach safety → reveal.** Plus a Speed stat kids understand.

## Phase 1 — Boss Heist (D22)
- **Where:** the reserved "BOSS VAULT · SOON" plot at the end of Main Street (Zone 1). Replace the sign with a big vault door and the robot's docking bay.
- **Timing:** the vault opens every 10 minutes for 60 seconds. Server-wide countdown on the HUD ("Vault opens in 3:20") and a sign at the vault. Siren + flashing lights when it opens.
- **Grab:** during the open window, each player can grab one Boss Capsule from the vault (one per player per opening). The player then carries it like a worker.
- **The boss: giant security robot** (original design, kid-friendly, not scary). It wakes when the vault opens and chases the nearest carrier back toward the Zone 1 exit.
  - Attacks are slow and telegraphed (≥1.5 s warning): a searchlight that locks on, a laser sweep along a red warning line on the ground, and a stomp shockwave you can jump over.
  - Players have 2 hits: first hit = shield bubble pops, second hit = capsule flies back into the vault. No damage, no death, keep all cash and everything else.
  - Robot speed ≈ base carry speed, so dodging and route matter; faster players escape more easily but can still be caught.
- **Safe:** crossing the Zone 1 exit line (safe zone) → capsule opens with the big reveal → worker goes to the player like a normal catch.
- **Boss Capsule odds:** guaranteed Epic or better, small Legendary/Secret chances. Put them in `HeistConfig` with the interval, window, robot speed and hit count. Odds shown on the vault sign.
- **Safety:** server-authoritative; one capsule per player per opening, saved once (idempotent by opening ID + player); can't be duplicated by rejoining, re-sending or dying. Disconnecting while carrying → capsule returns to the vault (nothing gained, nothing lost).
- **Teamwork is allowed:** friends can distract the robot; the robot only targets carriers.
- Feature flag `HeistEnabled` (Studio attribute). OFF = Main Street as before.
- Expose a Studio-only function to force the vault open (for the trailer kit in 033).

## Phase 2 — Speed stat + trails (D24)
- HUD: a shoe icon + Speed number under the cash pill (same style as the HUD).
- **Trails** tab in the Store, cash only: 6 trails, each raising speed (e.g. +5% … +30%) with its own visual trail. Equip one at a time. Owned/equipped are saved as new optional fields (schema stays 3).
- Speed applies to walking and carrying. Cap the total boost so the heist and stealing stay fair.
- **Royale Race ignores speed:** every racer runs at the same fixed speed.
- Trail effects are client-side, cheap, with "Low effects" respected. Prices and boosts in `SpeedConfig` for my approval.
