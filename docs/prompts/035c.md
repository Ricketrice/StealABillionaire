## Fair-play rules (don't break)
- Every attack has a clear warning of at least 0.8 s and can be dodged with normal phone movement. Hard = more to watch, never unfair.
- Caught = lose only the vault creature, keep everything else. No death, no scary visuals.
- Robux never helps win. Speed trails (cash-only) already exist. Show odds for the Boss Capsule.
- Each zone's vault opens every 10 min for 60 s, staggered by 3 min so heists don't overlap.
