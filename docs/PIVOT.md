## One line
Climb a world of 6 elemental kingdoms, catch their creatures, make them earn coins at your base, steal from other players, and rob each kingdom's boss while it chases you.

## What changes in the reskin
- **Name:** Steal a Billionaire → Steal a Boss. Currency Cash → Coins (display only; save fields keep their names).
- **Workers → creatures** per kingdom, all rarities (extends D26). Worker IDs, rarities, odds and income stay; only looks and display names change.
- **Boss Heist:** each kingdom's boss guards a vault (the security robot becomes the Meadow boss first; others later as updates).
- **Royale Race → Dragon Race** at Dragon Peak. Same rules.
- **Ranks:** Peasant → Squire → Knight → Captain → Lord → Boss (same thresholds).
- **Gamepasses:** 2x Coins, Extra Slots, VIP (same perks).
