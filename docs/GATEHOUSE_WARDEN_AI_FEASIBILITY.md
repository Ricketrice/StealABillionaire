# Gatehouse Warden (TRELLIS.2 output): technical feasibility review (2026-10-17)
Input: `~/Pictures/035_preview/concept/GatehouseWarden_AI.glb` (generated by the owner in the free HF Space; not regenerated). No Studio changes, no new generation, nothing existing touched. Scripts: `tools/blender/ai/gw_*.py`. Renders/boards: `~/Pictures/035_preview/gw/` (`COMPARE_raw_vs_8k.png`, `seg/POSED_strip.png`, `raw_{pbr,alb}_*`, `strip_*`).

## What the raw file is
- 1 mesh, **95,759 tris**, 87,775 split verts (45k after welding), 1 material, two 1024 WebP textures (base colour + packed metal/rough; EXT_texture_webp). No skeleton, no vertex colours. Normalised to 1.0 tall (0.76 wide, 0.41 deep) with the crest fin included; faces -Y after Blender import.
- **Topology:** after welding it is one fused shell (92.6k tris) + 54 tiny floating specks. 1,669 non-manifold edges (>2 faces) and 383 boundary edges. UV is a 6.6k-chart atlas of tiny islands. Triangle soup, not edge-flow: no quads, no loops, not editable by hand.
- **Symmetry:** approximate (87% of sampled verts have a mirror partner within 0.6%); not exact. Mesh is hollow in places (nested contours at arms: outer plus inner surface).
- **Proportions:** very chibi: legs only 25% of height, head ~0.1 of height (excl. crest), shoulders 0.76 wide (pauldrons dominate). Arms hang close to the torso with a ~2 cm gap (visible at z 0.35-0.55); fused at the shoulders, hips and tassets. Crest is a thin tall fin.
- **Look:** matches the concept very well (crenellated crown, portcullis visor, turret pauldrons with crenellated rims, gate-arch emblem on blue enamel, tabard, big boots) and is clearly better than V1-V5. Weaknesses: texture is only 1024 across the whole body (soft/blurry visor bars, smudged weathering), the surface is lumpy rather than crisp, hands are rough, crest is a thin slab.

## Experiments (on copies)
1. **Optimise to ~8k (done):** `gw_optimise.py`. Weld, then a plain decimate stalls at ~14.1k (the non-manifold edges block collapses). Working recipe: weld, drop specks, **voxel remesh 0.003** (810k faces), Decimate collapse to **8,250 tris**, smart-UV unwrap 1024, bake albedo + normal + packed metal/rough from the 96k original (Cycles, cage 0.02, ray 0.09). Silhouette, crown, pauldrons, belt, tabard and boots survive and read well at phone distance; fine detail (visor bars, rivets, emblem relief) turns to soft texture. First try (voxel 0.004, cage 0.012) had black bake holes and was rejected; second is clean. Output `~/Pictures/035_preview/gw/opt8k_b/` (glb + 1024 maps). 8k is a ceiling-sized budget for ONE Epic (my 8,000 ceiling): fits, but no spare for shield/sword props.
2. **Separate and pose (done):** `gw_segment.py`. Sliced the 8.25k mesh into 15 R15-style rigid parts (Head 654, UpperTorso 1,722, LowerTorso 1,331, UpperArm ~1,240 each, LowerArm ~520, Hand ~75, UpperLeg ~400, LowerLeg ~240, Foot ~220 tris), capped holes, posed arms/leg/head. Result: it **works from game distance**: pauldrons travel with the arm, limbs bend plausibly. Problems: cuts follow face centroids so seams are ragged (torn slivers at tasset/hip and shoulder), caps have no texture (need dark fill), the parts are hollow, joint pivots must be fitted by hand to the weld rig, hands are tiny blobs.

## Biggest technical problems
1. Mesh is one fused, non-manifold, hollow triangle soup: needs the voxel-remesh + bake step for every use; no direct import-ready asset.
2. Segmentation needs proper clean seams (planar or hand-placed cuts hidden under armour lames), caps, and per-part pivots; currently ragged. Roughly an artist-day of Blender per character; scripted but not automatic.
3. 8k only works if shield/sword are separate props with their own budget, and the knight then spends the entire Epic ceiling. A 5-6k version is the safer target.
4. Texture: one 1024 map (3 channels needed: colour/normal/metal-rough). Roblox PBR (SurfaceAppearance) wants separate Roughness and Metalness maps (split the packed one). One 1024 set per MeshPart is fine, but 15 parts that share one atlas means each part must reference/re-bake its own. Verify Roblox per-MeshPart limits and texture sizes in current docs before building (not tested here).
5. Heroic proportions (6.5 studs) still need the Studio rig/animation/carry/pad test (blocked on a verified backup and Studio reconnect).
6. Style: strongly "AI-sculpted lumpy": acceptable for a premium look but different from crisp hand-made Roblox style; will not match the 9 other characters unless they follow the same route.

## Safest route to Roblox
1. Keep the AI mesh as the **design master** only; never import the raw 96k file.
2. Per character: voxel remesh, decimate to 5-6k (7-8k only for hero/Epic), bake albedo/normal/metal-rough, split into rig parts with hand-cleaned seams, weld to the existing CHUNK rig (no skinning needed), split sword/shield into separate low-poly props.
3. First proof in Studio on one isolated dummy (flag OFF, verified backup first): import the 15 parts, weld rig, run idle/walk/attack/carry; measure the visual drop in-game at phone distance.
4. Only if that passes: decide on the other 8 characters (each needs its own concept image + a free generation; the HF quota is ~1 run per account per day, so 8 characters = days, unless the owner finds more quota).
5. Fallback if the in-game test disappoints: use this knight only as the high-quality reference for hand-modelled armour parts.
